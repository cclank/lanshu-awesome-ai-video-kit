## 🆕 2026-10 新增（7 条）

### ve-021 · Veo 3.1 原生对白与口型同步写法（120ms lip-sync）
`veo-3` `dialogue-driven` `dialogue` `lip-sync` `rain` · [www.atlascloud.ai](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/ai-updates/veo-3.1-prompt-guide)

```
Dialogue Structure: "Character [Description] says '[Exact Quote],' [Tone Adjective], [Sync Timing]."

Example: A middle-aged detective says 'I told you not to come here,' weary and gravelly tone, 120ms lip-sync delay.

Use quotation marks for spoken lines: putting the exact dialogue in quotes (says, "...") gives the model a clear start and stop point. List ambient sounds (like rain tapping or cafe murmur) at the end of the prompt so the engine does not blend environmental noise into the main speech track.
```

> 💡 Veo 3.1 的 48kHz native audio 对白标准写法：角色描述+says+精确引语+语气+lip-sync 时延；与已有 Veo 3 对白条目不同，这是 3.1 专属的同步语法。
演示视频：https://www.youtube.com/embed/48jtRE42Bw0

### ve-022 · Veo 3.1 85mm 跟拍 + 脚步重量物理与音效绑定
`veo-3` `technique-snippet` `rain` `neon` · [www.atlascloud.ai](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/ai-updates/veo-3.1-prompt-guide)

```
Cinematography: 85mm medium tracking shot, smooth eye-level dolly backward, shallow depth of field.

Subject & Action: A handsome man... walks forward... His boots firmly contact the rain-slicked asphalt with clear weight and friction...

Environment: Rain-soaked cinematic urban alleyway, vibrant neon lights...

(原文含省略号为来源作者删节) Native audio note: explicit physical-contact wording (boots firmly contact... with clear weight and friction) forces the physics engine to render grounded weight and lets the native audio engine snap footstep sounds to the visual contact frames.
```

> 💡 新技巧：用物理接触动词（firmly contact with clear weight and friction）消除“太空步”并实现 action-sound binding；机位参数前置锁定空间一致性。
